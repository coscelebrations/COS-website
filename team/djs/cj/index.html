The Story

The DJ Who Grew Up on Stage

CJ is a wedding DJ at COS Celebrations based in St. Augustine, Florida. He started DJing at 15 years old under the mentorship of his older brother Spyro - a respected name in the New York DJ circuit - and 16 years later, he's still doing it full-time. Along the way he toured the world as a professional drummer, playing venues across Asia, Europe, and South America. That combination of turntable technique and live-performer instinct is what makes his weddings feel different from the DJ down the street.

Long Island born and now St. Augustine based, CJ brings big-city energy to Florida weddings without losing the coastal warmth couples come here for. He is the DJ couples request when they want a room that never sits down - and the one venue coordinators quietly recommend to their favorite planners.

Signature Style

What Sets CJ Apart

Reads the Room on Instinct

Sixteen years of live shows behind him. CJ does not run a pre-programmed setlist - he adjusts every transition based on what the crowd is actually doing in the moment. That is why guests describe his sets as feeling personal.

A Drummer's Ear for Rhythm

Years of professional drumming means he thinks in beats before he thinks in songs. Transitions are seamless, energy builds are timed to the second, and the dance floor moves as one.

MC Presence That Fits the Room

CJ handles introductions, toasts, and every mic moment with a calm confidence you notice only because nothing goes wrong. He never overpowers the moment - he sets it up for you.

Takes the Time to Know You First

Every couple gets a real conversation before the wedding - not a form. CJ builds his sets around your story, your must-plays, and the tracks that will pack your specific dance floor.

Common Questions

Booking CJ & Working With Him

Can I request DJ CJ specifically for my wedding?

Yes. When you book with COS Celebrations, you can request CJ by name during your consultation. Availability is based on your wedding date - CJ books early for peak season weekends in St. Augustine and Jacksonville.

How long has CJ been DJing weddings?

CJ has 16 years in the wedding industry. He started at 15 under the mentorship of his brother Spyro, a respected DJ in the New York circuit, and has been performing full-time ever since.

What venues has CJ performed at in North Florida?

CJ regularly DJs at River House, The Treasury on the Plaza, The White Room, The Lightner Museum, and other premier wedding venues across St. Augustine and Jacksonville.

Does CJ handle MC duties as well as DJing?

Yes. CJ is a full DJ and MC. He handles introductions, toasts, dance floor cues, and every transition throughout the night. Couples consistently comment on how he reads the room and adjusts on the fly.

What makes CJ different from other wedding DJs?

CJ brings a drummer's ear for rhythm from years of touring internationally as a live drummer, combined with big-city energy from his New York background. He genuinely takes the time to get to know each couple before their wedding and builds every set around what makes their story unique.
