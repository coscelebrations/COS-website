Wedding Uplighting
Programmed, Not Just Placed

The cheapest way to change a room is to change its color. The hard part is knowing when to change it.

500+ five-star reviews • Color-matched on site • Jacksonville • St. Augustine • Ponte Vedra

COS Celebrations provides wedding uplighting across Northeast Florida, including Jacksonville, St. Augustine, Ponte Vedra Beach and Amelia Island. Uplighting is a set of LED fixtures placed around the perimeter of a reception room and aimed up the walls, so the color washes the architecture rather than the guests. COS Celebrations color-matches the fixtures on site to the couple's palette and runs them from the same booth as the music, so the color can shift between dinner, the first dance and the dance set.

Look at the third and fourth photos on this page. Same country club, same ballroom, same columns. One is purple, one is coral.

That is the entire argument for uplighting, and it is a better argument than any description of the equipment. Nothing was built, moved, rented or draped. The room changed color and became a different room.

Which is also why I push back gently when uplighting gets treated as a box to tick. Picking one color in March and leaving it up for six hours is the version most couples get, and it works - it just leaves most of the value on the table. The room should not look the same during your grandmother's dinner conversation as it does at 10:40pm.

So the question I actually ask is not what color. It is when does it change, and to what.

How We Build It

A Reception Has Four Lighting Moments

Cocktail hour

Low and warm, closer to candlelight than to color. Guests are talking and standing, and the room is competing with whatever daylight is left through the windows. This is the one couples skip and then miss in the photos.

Dinner

Your palette, held steady and kept soft. Every seated portrait your photographer takes for the next ninety minutes is lit by this decision, which is why we set it with your linens and florals already on the tables rather than guessing from a swatch.

First dance

The room drops back and the two of you get the contrast. This is the moment where uplighting and the dance floor rig have to agree with each other, because a bright moving head pointed at the wrong wall flattens the whole frame.

The dance set

Saturated, and now it is allowed to move. This is the only part of the night most people picture when they hear the word uplighting, and it is the shortest of the four.

Two Different Jobs

Uplighting or Dance Floor Lighting?

These get quoted together and talked about as one thing. They do opposite jobs, and knowing which one you actually care about is the difference between a good lighting budget and a wasted one.

Uplighting

Static color on the walls. It is architectural, it is in every wide photo of the room, and it is what people mean when they say the reception felt like something. It does its best work before the dancing starts.

Moving heads and floor lighting

The moving, beat-driven layer aimed at the floor. It is doing nothing for you during dinner and everything for you after the toasts. It photographs as energy rather than as mood.

Questions

Wedding Uplighting FAQ

What is uplighting at a wedding reception?

Uplighting is a set of LED fixtures placed on the floor around the perimeter of a room, aimed up the walls so the light washes the architecture instead of pointing at your guests. COS Celebrations sets it up at weddings across Northeast Florida, and the point of it is simple: it changes the color of the entire room without changing anything physical in it.

Can you match uplighting to our wedding colors?

Yes. The fixtures are color-mixing LEDs, so we are not choosing from a handful of gels - we dial the color in on site, in the actual room, with your linens and florals already in place. Bring a photo of the palette or a bridesmaid dress. Rooms lie about color until the lights are actually up, which is why we set it before guests arrive and adjust.

Does the uplighting stay the same color all night?

It does not have to, and on most of our weddings it does not. A common shape is warm and low through dinner, then a shift into your accent color when the dance floor opens, then something more saturated for the last hour. Because the lighting is run from the same booth as the music, the color changes land on the moment rather than at a random point in a song.

What is the difference between uplighting and dance floor lighting?

Uplighting is static architectural color on the walls - it sets the mood of the room. Dance floor lighting is the moving, beat-reactive layer over the floor itself. COS Celebrations packages describe these separately for a reason: uplighting is what your dinner photos look like, and moving heads are what the dance set looks like. Most couples want both, at different moments.

How much does wedding uplighting cost in Jacksonville?

COS Celebrations quotes uplighting as an add-on to a DJ package that starts at $1,500 rather than as a separate lighting rental. Most Northeast Florida couples land between $1,500 and $3,500 all in, depending on hours, ceremony coverage, and what else they add - live saxophone, cold sparks, a photo booth. The room size drives the lighting side of the number.

Do you provide uplighting at our venue, or does the venue?

Ask your venue first, because some already own a system and a few build it into their packages. If yours does, we would rather coordinate with it than sell you a second set of lights that fights it. If it does not, we bring ours. Either way we walk the room beforehand so the fixtures land on the walls worth lighting and not in a traffic path.
