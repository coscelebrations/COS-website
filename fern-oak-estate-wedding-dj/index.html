Here is the thing about Fern Oak Estate that changes how we quote it. Thirty-two of your guests sleep on the property. Behind a gate, on 15 fenced acres, with nobody's car keys in play.

At a hotel ballroom the night ends because the room ends. Here it does not. The last song plays and your people walk two hundred feet to a house they are staying in. So the real question at this venue was never "how long is the reception" — it is which parts of a whole weekend need sound, and which parts are better off with a speaker in a corner and nobody running them.

Fern Oak Estate sits on the shore of a private spring-fed lake in Pomona Park, Florida, a rural Putnam County town about 40 miles from St. Augustine and 70 from Jacksonville. Four separate residences — the Estate House, the Lake House, the Loft, and the Cottage — sleep 32 between them. The wedding side is built for gatherings of up to 80 people, with two outdoor kitchens and bar areas, an open lawn down to the water, and century-old live oaks with fern trails running under them.

The detail that gets our attention: the estate's own description lists two gazebos that can be used as stage areas. Not a corner of a ballroom, not a riser we bring. Actual covered structures at the edge of a lake, already built, already positioned. That is a very specific invitation to put live music somewhere it will be remembered.

COS Celebrations is a preferred vendor at Fern Oak Estate, listed on the estate's own preferred partner directory. We are a St. Augustine wedding DJ and live music company with 500+ five-star reviews across Northeast Florida, and packages here start at $1,500. We have worked weddings on this property, and it is genuinely not like working a ballroom — the day is spread over acres instead of stacked in one room, and the planning reflects that.

Worth Knowing Before You Book Anyone

Ask Fern Oak for Their Partner List Early

The estate publishes a preferred partner directory and its team will send it to you. We are on it, which means you can book us without clearing an approval step — but that is not the reason to ask for it.

Ask for it because it covers caterers, florists, rentals, coordinators and bakers as well, and everyone on it has already been on the property. At a venue this spread out, a vendor who has walked the grounds before your wedding day is worth more than one who has not.

Why Fern Oak Estate Couples Book COS

A Canopy Eats Sound. A Field Does Not.

The ceremony clearing sits under a century of oak growth with moss hanging into the frame. All that leaf and moss absorbs sound instead of letting it travel, so one system pushed from the back row does not reach the front rows evenly. We set closer and aim across the seating, and the floor is sand and shell rather than concrete — which means proper base plates, not a stand pushed into soft ground.

Ceremony and Reception Are a Walk Apart

Your vows are in the oak hammock. Your dinner is on the lakefront terrace. Running one system between the two means somebody is carrying speakers past your guests during cocktail hour. We bring a self-contained, battery-powered ceremony rig instead, so the clearing needs no power drop and nothing gets torn down while people still have drinks in their hands.

The Weekend Is the Event

With 32 people staying on site, the wedding is usually the middle day of three. We would rather help you decide up front which of those moments actually want a DJ and which just want a speaker and a playlist — a Friday welcome on the terrace often does; a Sunday morning by the lake usually does not. Sorting it early is cheaper than adding it in the last month.

Two Gazebos, Already Built as Stages

The estate calls them stage areas itself, and that is an opening most venues do not give you. Our founder toured 35+ countries playing saxophone with artists like Usher and Alicia Keys before starting COS. A horn playing from a lakeside gazebo while your guests are on the terrace is a genuinely different thing from the same solo in a function room.

Common Questions About Fern Oak Estate Weddings

Can we bring our own DJ to Fern Oak Estate?

Yes, and there is no approval step to clear first. COS Celebrations is listed on Fern Oak Estate's own preferred partner directory, so you can book us directly. Ask the estate's team for the current version of that list early anyway — it covers caterers, florists, rentals and coordinators too. We are based in St. Augustine, about 40 miles away, so there is no long-haul travel to price into your quote.

How much does a wedding DJ cost at Fern Oak Estate?

COS Celebrations packages start at $1,500 for a four-hour reception with DJ, MC, and premium sound. Fern Oak weddings usually run above that starting number, because the ceremony happens in the oak hammock and the reception happens on the lakefront terrace, which is two separate sound setups rather than one. Couples who also want music for a Friday welcome party land higher again. Most Fern Oak Estate couples end up between $2,000 and $3,500 once ceremony sound, cocktail hour, and lighting are included.

How many guests can Fern Oak Estate hold?

Fern Oak Estate is designed for wedding gatherings of up to 80 people, and its two outdoor kitchen and bar areas can host up to 80 guests. Separately, the four residences on the property sleep up to 32 overnight guests: the Estate House with five bedrooms, the Lake House with three, the Loft which sleeps four, and the Cottage which sleeps six. Those are two different numbers and it is worth keeping them apart when you build a guest list.

Does a wedding DJ at Fern Oak Estate need to cover more than the reception?

Often, yes, and it is the question most couples do not think to ask. Because 32 guests sleep on the property behind a gate, nobody is driving home at the end of the night and the celebration tends to run across a whole weekend rather than a single five-hour reception. Many Fern Oak couples want sound for a Friday welcome gathering or a Sunday send-off in addition to the wedding day itself. It is cheaper to plan that up front than to add it on in the final month, and some of it does not need a DJ at all — a good speaker and a playlist covers a lakeside morning perfectly well.

Where does the ceremony happen at Fern Oak Estate?

Ceremonies at Fern Oak Estate are held outdoors in the oak hammock, on a sand and shell clearing under century-old live oaks hung with Spanish moss, with ferns and native planting around the aisle. The estate also has two gazebos that its own team describes as usable stage areas, and an open lawn running down to the spring-fed lake. There is no indoor ceremony room, so a weather plan is a real conversation rather than a formality.

How do you run sound in an oak hammock rather than an open field?

A dense oak canopy behaves very differently from open pasture. Sound gets absorbed by the leaves and the moss rather than travelling, so a ceremony under the oaks needs speakers placed closer to the guests and aimed across the seating rather than a single system pushed from the back. The floor is sand and shell, not concrete, so speaker stands need proper plates rather than being pushed into soft ground. We also run the ceremony as its own battery-powered system, because the clearing is a walk from the nearest permanent power.
